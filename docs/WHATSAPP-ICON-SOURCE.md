# WhatsApp contact mark

The white WhatsApp glyph in `components/FloatingWhatsApp.tsx` is the unchanged brand path from `simple-icons@16.35.0`, `icons/whatsapp.svg` (npm registry tarball SHA1 `78db83dd7f5ae0212b68d05c2ae7797e5d864921`). Simple Icons distributes its icon artwork under CC0 1.0 Universal. WhatsApp trademarks remain with their owner; using the contact shortcut does not imply endorsement.

Only this glyph was copied, not the whole icon library. No new runtime dependency, remote logo request or generated/redrawn logo. Standard white 38px mark on #25D366; the rejected dark inner circle, halo and extra white outer ring were removed. An ongoing gentle 1.8-second bounce keeps the 56px link hit area stationary. A separate pause/resume button sits beside the logo and works with keyboard or pointer. Hover/focus/drag or reduced motion disable movement; without JavaScript the contact mark stays static.
