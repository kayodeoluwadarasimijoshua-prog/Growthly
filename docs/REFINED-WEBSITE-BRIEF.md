Build on Growthly's existing Next.js website and owner content editor. Make the site easier for Nigerian business owners to understand, evaluate and contact, while preserving the green identity, confirmed contact numbers, approved starting prices and first-year domain/hosting terms.

## Website changes

1. Update affected production dependencies to appropriate patched releases and verify the result.
2. Make the homepage offer direct and reduce repeated explanations. Keep real project evidence, all service destinations, clear website options, approved prices, process, FAQs and a useful next step.
3. Help visitors reach the actual quote form promptly, especially on mobile. Explain the current WhatsApp handoff before collecting details and retain truthful delivery states, validation, service context and privacy-safe fallbacks.
4. Keep Growthly work prominent on the portfolio. Separate independent inspiration from delivered projects.
5. Add a factual team section to About and remove repeated generic process copy where it adds no new information.

## Owner editing

Extend the existing private owner editor rather than build a separate CMS. It should support team names, roles, short bios, optional photos, ordering, hiding/removing profiles and starter templates. Preserve existing project/image, page-text, FAQ, contact and pricing editing.

Use private drafts, explicit publication, conflict protection and history restoration. Require review of profile information and separate photo permissions, alt text and source credit. Block unregistered image references. A withdrawn profile/photo must stop being served publicly. Restore history as a draft with permissions requiring review again. Older documents without team data must remain readable without resurrecting deleted people.

The owner has permitted StarXCO screenshot publication. A genuine screenshot still needs to be obtained and checked; do not replace it with generated or fabricated imagery. Use the existing text case study until valid image bytes are available.

## Boundaries and completion

Keep credentials out of chat, source and public bundles. Test with isolated mock data when real provider access is unavailable; state that limitation clearly. Real persistent editing requires the actual Supabase project/Auth/Storage setup. Do not create a fake login or claim localStorage is an online content database.

Do not deploy, purchase services, change DNS, activate advertising/analytics or grant staff accounts without the relevant explicit authorization. Finish implementation with a production build, appropriate unit/integration and browser coverage, accessibility/responsive review, SEO checks and clear remaining owner actions. Report measured results separately from hypotheses and provider acceptance.

The audit and implementation are intended to improve clarity and decision-making; they do not guarantee sales, search rankings or advertising returns.
